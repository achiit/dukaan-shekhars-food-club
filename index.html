From the owner

A new kitchen with an old habit: cook light

I opened Shekhar's Food Club this year on NH115, right opposite the Sadiya Police Station. For a long time I wanted a place where people from Chapakhowa could eat well without feeling heavy afterwards.

Most roadside food swims in oil. I wanted my biryani and fried rice to taste rich but sit light, the kind of meal you can have for lunch and still go back to work.

We are new and every plate still matters to me. If something is not right, tell me directly and I will fix it.

  • Less oil in every dishHealthy cooking is the whole idea, not an extra.
  • Biryani that people come back forIt is the dish most customers mention first.
  • Half and full platesEat what you need, pay for what you eat.
